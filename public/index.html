Infinite Minesweeper

Endless Minesweeper, no borders

Infinisweeper is a free browser minesweeper with no fixed board size — the grid keeps expanding in every direction as you explore. Reveal safe tiles, flag mines, and pan or zoom as far as you like. One wrong click ends the run, so submit your score and climb the global leaderboard. No download, no account, unlimited minesweeper straight from the browser.

How to play Infinisweeper

Left-click reveals a cell, right-click or the flag toggle marks a mine, and WASD, the arrow keys or dragging pans the endless board. Each safe cell is worth one point; reveal as many as you can without detonating a mine. Press C to jump to the center or L to return to your last click.

Frequently asked questions

Is Infinisweeper free to play?
Yes — it's a free in-browser game with no download, install, or account required.
How is it different from classic Minesweeper?
The board is infinite: instead of a fixed grid, it expands endlessly in every direction, so there's no set size to clear — you explore as far as you dare.
Is there a leaderboard?
Yes. Submit your score to compete on the live global leaderboard against other players.
Can I play on mobile?
Yes. Infinisweeper works in any modern mobile browser with touch panning and a flag/dig mode toggle.
